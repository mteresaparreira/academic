Another project to which I devote a considerable amount of my free time is Reboot, an NGO devoted to Sustainability. Our statement:

> We teach Sustainability in a simple, accessible and digital way. We understand we are all passionate about different things, but we are committed to including every single person in the fight against climate change. Different speeds, but the same goal.

I have been associated with Reboot since its foundation, in October/2020, and have taken on many interesting challenges which fall outside of my formal education. Pushing myself out of the box for a subject that I am so passionate about (Education for Awareness) has been incredibly rewarding. My functions include:

- Curating speakers and moderating events (such as an interactive conference on Ecotourism);
- Producing a documentary (including interviewing experts on food waste);
- Writing a monthly newsletter on Sustainability;
- Producing and hosting a podcast, [Sonstentável](https://open.spotify.com/show/6aWQnUkuPJRhIIc0E981ym){:target="_blank"};
- ...

I have the privilege of being part of a wonderful team of amazingly-talented volunteers which bring our ideas to life. I am eager to see this project bloom into materialized impact.
